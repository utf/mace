# D-SCC plan: v4.4 amendment — required versus optional electrostatics (apply to §2.4, §7)
- `K_LR` (the periodic long-range kernel at full weight) is REQUIRED physics: fixed
  coefficient from `eps_inf` and the exact monopole, validated by the tiling-ladder gate
  (§8). It is not subject to the equivalence rule and is present in every production
  configuration.
- Optional, subject to the equivalence rule: `lambda_dir`, `U_eff`, Route B′ (and its
  global scale). "Off" is a legitimate selected value for each.
- Arm 2+3 factorial: the "Φ = 0" arms are retained as force references only, not as
  candidate production models; the minimal candidate is "LR-only".
- Record: force-only selection cannot resolve the image term; its absence from the
  admissible tests is a data limitation, not evidence against it.
